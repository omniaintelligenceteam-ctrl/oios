# Confidence Kit — Objection Handling & FAQ

## The Mindset
You're not selling "AI." You're selling **time, money, and peace of mind.**

You don't need to be a tech expert. You need to understand their pain better than they do — and you already do, because you've lived it for 10 years.

When they ask a tech question you don't know, say: **"Great question — let me get you the specifics on that and follow up. But here's what it means for you in practice..."** Then redirect to the business outcome.

---

## Top 10 Objections + Responses

### 1. "I'm not tech-savvy."
**Your response:** "That's the whole point. You don't touch any of this. We set it up, it runs, you get the results. It's like hiring a really good office manager — you don't need to know how they do their job, you just need them to do it."

### 2. "What if the AI messes up?"
**Your response:** "There's always a human fallback. AI handles the 80% that's routine — answering calls, sending follow-ups, updating records. The 20% that needs judgment still comes to you or your team. Think of it as a filter, not a replacement."

### 3. "I already have [ServiceTitan / Jobber / CRM]."
**Your response:** "Good — OIOS doesn't replace that. It's the AI layer on top. It makes your existing tools work harder. Right now your CRM only knows what someone manually types in. With OIOS, it gets updated automatically from every call, every email, every interaction."

### 4. "$2-3K a month is a lot."
**Your response:** "What's your average office admin cost you? $3,500 a month plus benefits, sick days, training time? OIOS does 80% of that work for less than half the price. And it works nights, weekends, and holidays. The math is pretty simple."

### 5. "How long does setup take?"
**Your response:** "Three weeks. Week one, we audit your operations and identify what to automate first. Week two, we build and configure everything. Week three, we go live and monitor. You don't have to do anything except give us access to your systems."

### 6. "What if I want to cancel?"
**Your response:** "Month to month. No long-term contract. If you don't see value in 60 days, walk away. I'm betting you won't want to, though — nobody wants to go back to doing this stuff manually."

### 7. "Can't I just hire someone?"
**Your response:** "You could. But a good admin costs $40-50K a year, takes weeks to train, calls in sick, and only works 40 hours. OIOS costs $24-36K a year, is up in 3 weeks, works 24/7, and never forgets to follow up. Plus it scales — when you grow, OIOS grows with you without hiring again."

### 8. "How is this different from other AI tools?"
**Your response:** "Most AI tools do one thing — answer the phone, or generate a proposal, or track data. OIOS does all of it as one system. The real magic is that everything talks to everything. A call comes in, the AI answers, the data goes to your CRM, a proposal gets generated, follow-ups are scheduled, and your dashboard updates — all automatically. Nobody else packages it like this."

### 9. "I need to talk to my partner/wife about it."
**Your response:** "Absolutely. Want me to put together a one-pager you can show them? It'll have the ROI numbers and exactly what's included. Sometimes it's easier to see it on paper than explain it."

### 10. "This sounds too good to be true."
**Your response:** "I get that. Here's what I'd say — I run a service business too. I built this because I was drowning in the same admin work you are. It's not magic — it's just automating the stuff that shouldn't need a human in the first place. The demo shows exactly what it does, nothing more. What you see is what you get."

## Power Phrases to Use in Demos

- "Let me show you what happens when someone calls your business at 8 PM on a Tuesday."
- "Right now, this lead is on a sticky note somewhere. With OIOS, it's already in your system."
- "How many jobs do you think you lose each month to missed calls?"
- "What would you do with an extra 15 hours a week?"
- "This is what your Monday morning looks like with OIOS — everything organized, nothing missed."
- "You could be on a beach and know exactly how your business is doing."

---

## Questions to Ask THEM (Flip the Script)

These put you in control and make them sell themselves on OIOS:

- "How many calls do you think you miss in a week?"
- "What happens when someone calls after 5 PM?"
- "How long does it take you to write a proposal right now?"
- "When was the last time a lead went cold because nobody followed up?"
- "If you could see one number about your business right now, what would it be?"
- "What would you do with an extra day and a half each week?"

---

## If They Ask Something You Don't Know

**Don't fake it.** Say one of these:

- "That's a great detail question — let me get you the specifics on that and follow up by tomorrow."
- "I'll check on the exact specs for that and send it over. But functionally, here's what it does for you..."
- "We customize everything to your setup, so the answer depends on your specific tools. Let me map that out after the demo."

Then actually follow up. This builds more trust than a BS answer.

## The 30-Second Elevator Pitch

*Use this when someone asks "So what do you do?"*

> "I built an AI system that runs the back office for service businesses. It answers your phone 24/7, handles proposals and follow-ups automatically, and gives you a live dashboard of your whole operation. Basically, it's like hiring an office manager that works around the clock for less than half the cost. I'm working with [landscape lighting / HVAC / service] companies right now."

## Demo Flow (15-20 Minutes)

1. **Open with their pain** (2 min) — "How many calls do you miss a week? What happens after hours?"
2. **Show the AI Receptionist** (4 min) — Side-by-side: voicemail vs. AI answering and booking
3. **Show the data extraction** (2 min) — "From that one call, the AI got all this"
4. **Show the Back Office** (4 min) — Proposals in 30 seconds, automated follow-ups
5. **Show the Command Center** (3 min) — "Ask it how your business is doing"
6. **Show the full loop** (2 min) — Everything connected, nothing falls through
7. **The offer** (2 min) — Founding member, $2K/mo, 60-day walk-away guarantee
8. **Close or next step** (1 min) — "Want one of the founding member spots?"
