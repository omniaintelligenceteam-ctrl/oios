## Products & Services

### OIOS (Omnia Intelligence Operating System)

**One-liner:** A $1,500-5,000/month AI operations partner that handles calls, automates admin, and gives business owners a real-time command center — replacing the work of a full-time office manager.

**Positioning:** Between cheap AI receptionists ($100-300/mo) and expensive AI agencies ($15K+/mo). Nobody else occupies the full back-office AI slot at $1.5-5K/mo.

#### The 3 Pillars

**Pillar 1: AI Receptionist — "Never Miss a Lead"**
- 24/7 AI phone answering (Retell AI — 600ms response, best-in-class)
- Lead capture, qualification, scoring
- Appointment booking (direct calendar sync)
- After-hours + holiday coverage
- Call transcripts + sentiment analysis
- Setup: <1 week

**Pillar 2: AI Back Office — "Kill the Paperwork"**
- Automated follow-ups after quotes/jobs
- Proposal and invoice generation from templates
- Client onboarding sequences (3-4 hours → 15 minutes)
- Email triage, response drafting, scheduling
- CRM updates and data entry
- Setup: 2-5 days per workflow

**Pillar 3: AI Command Center — "See Everything"**
- Real-time business dashboard (pipeline, revenue, jobs, crew)
- Natural language queries: "How's my pipeline this week?"
- Daily/weekly AI-generated briefings
- Proactive alerts: "3 proposals stalled — want me to follow up?"
- Suggested actions based on data patterns
- Setup: 3-7 days

#### What OIOS Does NOT Do
- Replace complex judgment calls
- Handle emotionally sensitive conversations
- Replace specialized software (QuickBooks, ServiceTitan) — it integrates with them
- Run fully autonomously without any human review

#### Target Customer
Service business owners doing $500K-$5M/year with 5-50 employees. Home services, trades, manufacturing. Owners who miss leads, spend 15-20 hrs/week on admin, and want to scale but are stuck being the bottleneck.

#### Tech Stack
- **Intelligence:** Claude Code — CLAUDE.md (brain), 10 pre-built skills, file-based CRM, context system, self-improvement engine
- **Orchestration:** OpenClaw — Telegram bot, webhook receiver, cron scheduler, message routing, credential storage
- **Voice:** Retell AI — 600ms response, inbound call handling, transcript delivery via webhook
- **Client interface:** Telegram — client texts OIOS like texting an assistant
- **Admin interface:** Wes manages each instance via Claude Code directly
- **Architecture:** OpenClaw = nervous system (routing/scheduling), Claude Code = brain (intelligence/data). Client gets their own instance.
