# Brilliance Outdoor Lighting

## Company Info
- **Owner:** Jake Harmon
- **Role:** Founder / CEO
- **Location:** Dallas, TX
- **Founded:** 2019
- **Revenue:** $1.8M (2025)
- **Employees:** 12
  - 3 sales reps
  - 6 install crew (2 teams of 3)
  - 1 office manager (Lisa)
  - 1 lighting designer
  - Jake (sells, manages, puts out fires)

## What They Do
- Residential landscape lighting — patios, driveways, walkways, accent trees, architectural lighting
- Avg job: $4,000-$12,000
- Service area: Dallas-Fort Worth metro (Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Southlake, Highland Park)
- Peak season: March-October
- 80% residential, 20% commercial (restaurants, HOAs)

## Current Tools
- QuickBooks for invoicing
- Google Calendar (barely used)
- Group text for crew scheduling
- Whiteboard in the office for job tracking
- Lisa answers phones, takes notes on sticky notes
- Facebook page (rarely updated)
- No CRM, no automation, no AI

## Pain Points
1. **Missing 40% of inbound calls** — Lisa can only answer one at a time, nothing after hours
2. **No follow-up system** — Jake says "I'll call them back" and forgets
3. **Proposals take 2 hours each** — copy-paste from old Word docs, manually adjust
4. **Scheduling is chaos** — whiteboard + group text, double-bookings happen monthly
5. **No way to track leads** — sticky notes get lost, no pipeline visibility
6. **Zero online presence** — no LinkedIn, Facebook posts once a quarter
7. **Post-install follow-up is nonexistent** — no review requests, no referral asks

## Revenue Impact of Problems
- 40% missed calls x 20 calls/day x 5 days = 40 missed calls/week
- Even if 25% are serious inquiries = 10 hot leads/week lost
- At $5K avg job and 30% close rate = $15K/week = **$60K/month in lost revenue**

## Why They're a Perfect Demo Target
- Every pain point is solvable with AI
- The ROI is massive and easy to calculate
- Jake is tech-curious but not technical — needs someone to set it up
- This mirrors hundreds of service businesses in every city
