# Proposal Writer

Generate service proposals for prospects — clear, confident, and built around their specific situation.

## Trigger
User says things like: "write a proposal for [company]", "draft a proposal for [prospect]", "they want a proposal"

## Process

### Step 1: Gather info
Ask Wes (if not provided):
- Company name and what they do
- What service are we proposing? (Silent AI Partner, custom implementation, etc.)
- What pain points came up in the demo or call?
- Any pricing discussed or should I leave it as a placeholder?
- Timeline discussed?
- Any specific deliverables or outcomes they asked about?

### Step 2: Draft the proposal

**Format:**
```
# Proposal: AI Implementation for [Company Name]
**Prepared for:** [Name], [Title] — [Company]
**Prepared by:** Wes | Omnia Intelligence AI
**Date:** [date]

---

## The Problem
[2-3 sentences describing their specific pain in their words. Show you listened.]

## What We're Building Together
[Short description of the engagement — what it is and what it's not]

## What You Get

### [Deliverable 1]
[One-liner description]

### [Deliverable 2]
...

## How It Works
[Simple 3-5 step process — what happens after they say yes]

1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
...

## Investment
[Pricing — or placeholder: "TBD based on scope conversation"]

## Timeline
[Estimated start and key milestones — or placeholder]

## Why Omnia Intelligence AI
[2-3 sentences. Not a brag — a reason to trust you with this.]

## Next Step
[Clear CTA — e.g., "Reply to this email", "Book a 30-min call", "Sign and return"]
```

### Step 3: Present + refine
- Show the draft
- Ask: "Want to adjust pricing, add/remove anything, or change the tone?"
- Offer a shorter "one-pager" version if needed

## Notes
- Proposals should feel like they were written for this company, not recycled.
- Keep it under 2 pages. CEOs don't read walls of text.
- The "Problem" section is the most important — if they don't feel seen, they won't read the rest.
- Save winning proposals (anonymized) to `references/examples/` as templates.
