# Cold Outreach

Draft cold emails or DMs to CEOs/decision-makers in Wes's voice — casual, confident, like a friend who found something they'd be crazy to ignore.

## Trigger
User says things like: "draft an outreach to [company/person]", "write a cold email to [CEO]", "help me reach out to [target]"

## Process

### Step 1: Gather info
Ask Wes (if not already provided):
- Who is the target? (name, title, company)
- What does their company do?
- What problem are they likely dealing with that AI could solve?
- Channel: email or DM?
- Any personal detail to reference? (mutual connection, recent news, LinkedIn post, etc.)

### Step 2: Draft the message

**Voice rules:**
- Sound like Wes — casual, direct, zero corporate fluff
- No "I hope this finds you well"
- No "I wanted to reach out because..."
- Open with something real and specific to them
- One sentence on what Omnia Intelligence AI does
- One sentence on the specific value/result they could get
- One low-friction CTA (a question, not a demand)
- Keep it under 150 words

**Email format:**
```
Subject: [short, specific, no clickbait]

[Opening hook — specific to them]

[What Omnia does in one sentence]

[Specific value for their situation]

[CTA — one simple question]

— Wes
Omnia Intelligence AI
```

**DM format:**
```
[Hook — even shorter, 1-2 sentences]
[Value prop]
[CTA]
```

### Step 3: Present + offer variations
- Show the draft
- Offer a more direct version and a softer version if Wes wants options
- Ask: "Want me to adjust the angle or tone?"

## Notes
- Always personalize. Generic = trash.
- If Wes gives you a list of targets, draft one at a time and check before moving to the next.
- Log successful outreach templates in `references/examples/` so we can reuse what works.
