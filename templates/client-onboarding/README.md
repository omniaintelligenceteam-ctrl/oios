# OIOS Client Onboarding Template

Use this template when onboarding a new OIOS client. Copy the entire folder and fill in the blanks.

## Steps

1. **Copy this folder** for the new client: `projects/{client-name}/context/`
2. **Fill L0 during discovery call** (~15 min with the client)
   - `L0-identity.md` — Who they are, what they do, #1 priority
   - `L0-communication.md` — How they talk, email signature, response expectations
3. **Fill L1 from discovery notes** (after the call)
   - `L1-business.md` — Products, services, pricing, competitors
   - `L1-goals.md` — What they want to achieve this quarter
4. **Fill L2 during setup week** (as you configure their OIOS instance)
   - `L2-priorities.md` — What they're focused on right now
   - `L2-tools.md` — Their current tools, integrations, credentials
   - `L2-team.md` — Who's on their team
5. **L3 and L4 emerge naturally** during operations
   - L3 = project-specific docs (per automation workflow)
   - L4 = session notes, scratch work

## What This Gives the Client

- Their AI knows who they are, how they talk, and what matters to them
- Context is layered — foundational stuff never gets lost, operational stuff stays fresh
- The self-improvement pipeline means their AI gets smarter with every correction
- Keyword mapping ensures the right context loads for the right task
